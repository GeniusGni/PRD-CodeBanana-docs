## **What is Team Plan**

Team Plan is a flexible organization subscription that centralizes seats, compute resources, AI usage, and administration in CodeBanana.

It allows organizations to manage:

- Customizable seat quantities
- Seat-specific VM configurations
- A shared **Budget Balance**
- Member on-demand limits
- Available models and reusable model templates
- Organization-level billing and usage records

Unlike individual plans, Team Plan AI usage is funded through a shared organization balance rather than a separate included token quota for each seat.

---

## **Get Team Plan**

Team Plan can be purchased from **Pricing → Team Plan**.

<Frame>
  ![0418 Teamplan Pricing](/images/0418-teamplan-pricing.png)
</Frame>

### **Seats and Budget Balance**

Seats and AI usage balance are purchased separately.

- Seat quantity can be selected based on organization needs
- A minimum of **1 seat** is required
- Each seat costs **\$20 / month**
- AI usage is deducted from the organization’s shared **Budget Balance**
- Budget Balance starts from **\$100**
- Budget Balance does not expire and can be topped up later

### **Validity period**

Team Plan supports multiple validity periods:

- **1 month**
- **3 months**
- **6 months**
- **12 months**

Available discounts may vary depending on the selected validity period.

### **VM configurations**

Seats are associated with specific VM configurations.

Available VM tiers include:

- **2 vCPU / 4 GB RAM**
- **4 vCPU / 8 GB RAM**
- **8 vCPU / 16 GB RAM**

The available seat quantity is tracked separately for each VM configuration.

The **Usage** page provides organization-level management for Owners and Admins, while allowing members to view their own available usage and VM resources.

### **Usage Alerts**

CodeBanana provides Team Plan usage alerts for member on-demand limits and the shared organization Budget Balance.

**Member on-demand limit**

- A reminder is shown when a member’s remaining on-demand limit is **10% or less and no more than \$10**
- Each user receives this reminder at most **once per day**
- Reminders are evaluated independently for each user
- The user is asked to contact the organization Owner or Admin before usage is interrupted

**Reminder text**

> You're almost at your on-demand limit ({usedPct}% used). Reach out to your organization owner or admin to increase your limit before usage is interrupted.

**Shared organization Budget Balance**

- A reminder is shown when the remaining Budget Balance falls below **10%**
- The reminder is intended for the organization Owner or Admin so the balance can be topped up in time

**Reminder text**

> Your organization's shared token budget is almost depleted — \${remaining} left. Please contact your organization owner or admin to top up soon and keep your team running smoothly.

<Note>
  No reminder is shown when a member’s personal plan quota is exhausted while the organization still has available shared Budget Balance.
</Note>

### **Team usage (Admin view)**

Organization Owners and Admins can view:

- Seats used and available
- Seat availability by VM configuration
- Shared Budget Balance
- Organization-wide AI usage
- Member usage and on-demand limits
- Seat records
- Budget transaction history

They can also:

- Add seats
- Top up Budget Balance
- Renew Team Plan
- Open **Members & Quotas**
- Review seat and balance changes

### **Personal usage (Member view)**

Each Team Plan member can view:

- Current token usage
- Available on-demand limit
- Current VM configuration
- Limit reset time
- Team Plan expiration information available to them

For Team Plan members, AI usage is deducted from the organization’s shared Budget Balance, subject to the member’s configured on-demand limit.

## **Admin Controls**

Organization Owners and Admins can manage Team Plan configuration, members, billing behavior, models, limits, and VM resources.

Members must already belong to the organization before they can be added to Team Plan.

### **Billing mode**

Billing Mode determines whose usage identity is used when AI costs are attributed inside the organization.

Organization Owners and Admins can change the organization’s Billing Mode.

**Where to configure**

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner
2. Select **Billing Mode**
3. Choose a billing mode
4. Click **Save**

<Frame>
  ![0715 Billingmode](/images/0715-billingmode.png)
</Frame>

**Available billing modes**

- **Owner Mode** — Usage is attributed to the project Owner
- **Presenter Mode** — Usage is attributed to the current presenter based on their actual activity

<Note>
  The selected Billing Mode applies to the entire organization. The AI-Native Index Dashboard also displays usage statistics based on the selected mode.
</Note>

### **Model templates**

Team Plan admins can create reusable model configurations for different teams or types of work.

A model template can define:

- Available models
- Model display order
- Default model
- Whether **Auto mode** is used as the default

Examples include:

- Product and engineering template
- Marketing and operations template
- Cost-controlled template

After a template is created, admins can apply it when adding members or managing existing members in **Members & Quotas**.

### **Model usage limits**

Team Plan admins can control which AI models each member is allowed to use.

**Where to configure**

- Go to **Usage → Members & Quotas**
- Find the **Models** column
- Apply a model template or select models manually
- Configure one member or multiple members at once

<Frame>
  ![0418 Teamplan Modellimite Overview](/images/0520-teamplan-modellimite-overview.png)
</Frame>

1. **Per-member model limits**

For each member, admins can select:

- **All** — all organization-available models
- **Model template** — a reusable model configuration created by the admin
- **Selected models only** — only individually selected models are available

Models outside the configured range are unavailable to that member.

<Frame>
  ![0418 Teamplan Modellimite Single](/images/0520-teamplan-singlemodel.png)
</Frame>

2. **Batch member management**

Team Plan admins can manage multiple members at once from **Members & Quotas**.

Admins can:

- Filter members by department based on the organization structure
- Select multiple members or use **Select All**
- Set member on-demand token limits in bulk
- Apply available models or model templates in bulk

<Frame>
  ![0520 Teamplan Batchmodel](/images/0520-teamplan-batchmodel.png)
</Frame>

**Filtering and visibility**

- The **Model Limits** filter helps identify members with restricted model access
- The member table shows the model configuration applied to each member
- Unavailable models appear disabled in project model selectors

**Restriction scope**

- Model restrictions are applied at the member level
- If a restricted member creates a project, everyone in that project can only use the models allowed under that member’s model configuration

![0418 Teamplan Modellimit Result](/images/0715-modellimit.png)

### **Additional payment when seats are insufficient**

If the required VM seat is unavailable, confirming the member configuration redirects the admin to the payment page.

Payment can be completed through:

- **Budget Balance**, when the organization has sufficient balance
- **Stripe**, when additional payment is required

### **Member on-demand limits**

Organization Owners and Admins can configure an independent on-demand limit for each Team Plan member.

Admins can:

- Set limits while adding members
- Modify limits directly in **Members & Quotas**
- Configure multiple members in bulk
- Apply different limits to individual members

A member’s available AI usage depends on:

- Their configured on-demand limit
- The organization’s remaining Budget Balance

When the member approaches their limit, the Team Plan usage alert rules apply.

### **Modify member plans**

Admins can manage:

- On-demand limits
- Model permissions
- VM configurations
- Team Plan membership

**VM upgrades**

- VM upgrades are supported
- Changes take effect after confirmation
- If an additional seat or payment is required, the admin is directed to the payment flow
- Payment can use Budget Balance when sufficient, or Stripe

**VM downgrades**

- VM downgrades are supported
- Each member can downgrade their VM configuration only **once during an entitlement period**

**Remove member**

- Admins can remove members from Team Plan
- The released seat becomes available for reassignment
- Removing a member does not automatically reduce the number of purchased seats

## **Budget Balance**

**Budget Balance** is the organization’s shared balance for Team Plan usage and management.

It replaces the previous **Token Balance** name because the balance can now be used for more than AI token consumption.

Budget Balance can be used for:

- Organization-wide AI usage
- Team Plan renewal
- Purchasing additional seats
- Upgrading member VM configurations

### **Top up Budget Balance**

Organization Owners and Admins can top up Budget Balance from the Usage page.

Available preset amounts include:

- **\$100**
- **\$500**
- **\$1,000**
- **\$2,000**

A custom amount can also be entered.

The top-up panel shows:

- Current balance
- Top-up amount
- Balance after top-up

Budget Balance does not expire.

---

## **Add Seats**

Organization Owners and Admins can purchase additional seats at any time from the Usage page.

Additional seats can use different VM configurations, including:

- **2 vCPU / 4 GB RAM**
- **4 vCPU / 8 GB RAM**
- **8 vCPU / 16 GB RAM**

Payment can be completed through:

- Budget Balance, when sufficient
- Stripe

Additional seats follow the current Team Plan expiration date.

If a seat is added during an active Team Plan period, the seat fee is prorated based on the remaining time in the current period.

---

## **Renewal**

Team Plan can be renewed from the **Usage** page by clicking **Renew**.

During renewal, admins can:

- Select a validity period of **1, 3, 6, or 12 months**
- Increase seats
- Reduce seats
- Configure seat quantities for different VM tiers
- Pay with Budget Balance when sufficient
- Continue through Stripe when needed

<Warning>
  Before reducing the number of seats, remove the corresponding members from Team Plan so enough seats are available to remove.
</Warning>

Each Team Plan entitlement period can be renewed **only once** before expiration.

### **Seat details**

Admins can view:

- Purchased seats by VM configuration
- Used seats
- Available seats
- Seat changes

Seat records help admins understand current capacity and decide when additional seats are required.

### **Budget history**

- Top-up
- Team Plan renewal
- Additional seat purchase
- VM upgrade
- VM downgrade
- Refund or balance return

Income and expense records are visually distinguished to make balance changes easier to understand.

---

## **Transition Note**

The updated Team Plan changes how existing Team Plan token benefits are managed.

For existing Team Plan customers:

- Used Token in Plan and used Token Limit amounts are combined in the usage display
- Existing configured member Token Limits remain unchanged
- Unused **Token in Plan** balance is transferred to the organization’s **Budget Balance**
- The transferred balance becomes available to all Team Plan members
- The balance can also be used for renewal, additional seats, and VM upgrades

Existing Team Plan users retain the value of their unused benefits through this migration.

## FAQs

Answers to common questions about Team Plan.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where can I purchase Team Plan?">
    Team Plan can be purchased from **Pricing → Team Plan**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does each Team Plan seat include a separate token quota?">
    No. Seats and AI usage balance are purchased separately. Team members consume AI usage from the organization’s shared Budget Balance, subject to their configured on-demand limits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the minimum Budget Balance top-up?">
    Budget Balance starts from **\$100**. Preset amounts include \$100, \$500, \$1,000, and \$2,000, and custom amounts are also supported.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Budget Balance expire?">
    No. Budget Balance does not expire.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can Budget Balance be used for?">
    Budget Balance can be used for AI usage, Team Plan renewal, additional seat purchases, and member VM upgrades.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What validity periods are available for Team Plan?">
    Team Plan supports **1, 3, 6, and 12 months**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can different VM configurations be assigned to different members?">
    Yes. Seats are associated with VM configurations, and members can be assigned different VM tiers based on available seats.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can admins add seats with different VM configurations?">
    Yes. Additional seats can be purchased for 2 vCPU / 4 GB, 4 vCPU / 8 GB, or 8 vCPU / 16 GB configurations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I add a seat during an active Team Plan period?">
    The added seat follows the current Team Plan expiration date. Its seat fee is prorated based on the remaining time in the active period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can admins manage multiple members at once?">
    Yes. Admins can filter members by department, select multiple members, and configure on-demand token limits and model access in bulk.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can individual members be configured separately?">
    Yes. Admins can separately configure a member’s on-demand token limit, available models or model template, and VM configuration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when the required VM seat is unavailable?">
    CodeBanana redirects the admin to the payment page after confirmation. Payment can use Budget Balance when sufficient, or Stripe when additional payment is required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can VM configurations be downgraded?">
    Yes. VM downgrades are supported, but each member can downgrade only once during an entitlement period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reduce seats immediately?">
    Purchased seats are reduced during renewal. Before reducing seats, remove the corresponding members from Team Plan so enough seats are available to remove.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Budget Balance to renew Team Plan or purchase seats?">
    Yes. When the organization has sufficient Budget Balance, it can be used for Team Plan renewal, additional seats, and VM upgrades. Otherwise, payment can continue through Stripe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can admins filter members by department?">
    Yes. The Add Members and member management flows support filtering members based on the organization’s department structure.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can admins create reusable model configurations?">
    Yes. Admins can create model templates that define available models, model order, and the default model, including Auto mode.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can admins restrict which AI models members can use?">
    Yes. Admins can apply a model template or manually select the available models for individual members or multiple members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do member model restrictions affect projects?">
    If a restricted member creates a project, everyone in that project can only use the models allowed under that member’s model configuration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When will Team Plan show usage warnings?">
    Team Plan uses separate warning rules:

    - A member receives a reminder when their remaining on-demand limit is **10% or less and no more than \$10**
    - The organization Owner or Admin receives a reminder when the remaining Budget Balance falls below **10%**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often can a member receive an on-demand limit reminder?">
    Each user receives the reminder at most once per day. The reminder is evaluated independently for each Team Plan member.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can admins review seat and Budget Balance changes?">
    Admins can review seat details and Budget Balance history from the Usage page, including top-ups, renewals, additional seats, and VM changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to unused benefits from the previous Team Plan?">
    Unused Token in Plan value is transferred to the organization’s Budget Balance. It can then be shared by Team Plan members and used for eligible Team Plan payments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
